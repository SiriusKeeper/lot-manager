# Privacy Policy for the Lot Manager apps

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Princess Lot Manager and Sirius Lot Manager ("the apps") store the vehicle, sales and photo information you enter on your phone.

## Google Drive

When you sign in with Google, the apps use Google Drive access only to create, read and update files inside the shared "Princess Lot Manager" folder in your Drive. The apps do not read, change or delete any other files in your Drive. Google user data is used only to provide this sync and is never used for advertising, sold, or transferred to anyone else.

## Sharing

Your data is shared only with the people you share that Drive folder with. The apps do not send your data to the developer or to any third party, and contain no ads or analytics.

## VIN lookup

If you use "Fill in from VIN", the VIN you entered is sent to the U.S. government's free NHTSA vPIC service to look up the year, make and model. Nothing else is sent.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling an app removes its data from the phone. Data in Google Drive is removed by deleting the shared folder. You can remove the apps' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for these apps.
